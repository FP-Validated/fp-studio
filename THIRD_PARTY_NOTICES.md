# Third-party notices

FP Studio uses source from OpenWorker and the FP Infographic Agent Kit at the exact commits in UPSTREAMS.lock.json. The assembler retains their original LICENSE files and notices in the assembled source.

The renderer uses @resvg/resvg-wasm 2.6.2 (MPL-2.0). The dependency itself is installed on the build machine and its license/source obligations remain applicable to distributed builds. Preserve all installed dependency notices when packaging.

No font files, provider credentials, Apple signing material, third-party executables or complete upstream repositories are included in this source archive. The builder stages user-supplied fonts with their notices only on the user-controlled Mac.
